About Howard Burr

Biography

Howard Burr is an American artist working in sculpture, painting, drawing, and printmaking.

Education and Professional Background

Born 1953, Middlesex, Connecticut

1978 - BFA, University of Connecticut, Storrs, Connecticut

1980 - MFA, Maryland Institute College of Art, Baltimore, Maryland

1989 - MBA, New York University, New York, New York

1990–2026 - Founder and owner, Burr Graal Glass, Inc., New York, New York

Lives and works on the Connecticut shoreline.
